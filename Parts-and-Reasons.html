Pitch Axis Parts and Reasons

Muhammad Ayaan Ahmad · Team 103 · Revision 1.2 · 16 November 2026

All capacitors are non-polar. C1 and C7 require the specified bipolar electrolytic parts; the remaining capacitors are ceramic. A bipolar electrolytic is non-polar even though it uses an electrolytic construction.

Main IC: U2, PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano. JP7 is the new VOFF-to-ground jumper. JP1 remains the external 5 V-to-VTG jumper. J4 carries RX on pin 1, TX on pin 2 and ground on pin 8; the main-board directions require team agreement.
