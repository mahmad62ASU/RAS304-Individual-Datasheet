Bring it up one boundary at a time.

  1. Inspect before powering.

    Check connector polarity, fuse, regulator pinout, Nano power arrangement, and ground continuity. Keep the motor bus isolated.

  2. Verify the rails.

    Use a current-limited source. Measure 5 V across load and source tolerance, then record regulator surface temperature.

  3. Check each probe channel.

    Substitute known resistances, inspect divider/buffer/ADC values, and test open and short conditions.

  4. Calibrate temperature.

    Compare each mounted probe against a reference across the intended range. Document mounting gradients, tolerance and settling delay.

  5. Test the state machine.

    Exercise warning, trip, invalid sensor, cooling and manual rearm. Verify latches cannot clear while hot or invalid.

  6. Integrate the shared fault.

    Confirm open-collector behavior, one pullup, low-level voltage and startup inhibit. Scope pin 5 assertion to motor cutoff separately from sensor heating lag.

  7. Validate optional cooling.

    Measure fan startup and running current, switch temperature, supply dips and analog noise. Reassess source and fuse choices.
