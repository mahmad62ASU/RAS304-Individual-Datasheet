Before ordering.

  1. Confirm thermal mounting.

    Choose insulating probe attachment, cable strain relief, and sensor connector polarity.

  2. Close the power choices.

    Check adapter tolerance, fan start/stall current, reverse-protection drop, fuse coordination, and regulator thermal mounting.

  3. Validate the exact footprints.

    Verify the Nano headers and every transistor pinout against the selected manufacturer. Do not assume TO-92 pinouts are interchangeable.

  4. Capture a dated quote.

    Add unit and extended prices from the actual supplier cart, including shipping, tax, PCB fabrication, and spares.
