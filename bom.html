Quantity and assembly notes.

  • Normally one build-equivalent for breadboard work, one for the PCB and one spare. The 10k and 1k resistors, jumper headers/shunts, terminals and LEDs use cheaper quantity tiers with additional spares. Fuses are ordered in the supplier's five-piece minimum.
  • One regulated 9V adapter is reused for sequential prototype and PCB testing. Ribbon-cable quantity is three linear feet, not three reels. The three IDC sockets are for three local cable tails; confirm the team's far-end termination and pin-1 orientation.
  • Six 100nF capacitors are needed per build: three ADC filters, two op-amp bypass capacitors and one regulator output bypass. There is also one 330nF input bypass and two 10uF bulk capacitors. Resistors are axial 1%, 0.25W. Ceramic capacitors are 50V X7R; the 10uF electrolytics are 25V, 105C.
  • Three red status LEDs are proposed, each with a 1k resistor (approximately 3mA at nominal 5V and 2V LED drop). Label the indicator functions. The optional fan branch is outside this request.

Check before assembly.

Q1 substitution: the original P2N2222A is obsolete. Proposed KSP2222ABU has pin 1 emitter, pin 2 base, pin 3 collector; the original part has collector and emitter reversed. Update the schematic symbol and PCB footprint and wire by function.

Power: the PSC15A-090-R adapter is regulated 9V, 1.67A, center positive, with a 2.1mm-bore barrel plug. Match it to PJ-102A. Route jack positive through the 500mA time-delay fuse, then D4 anode to banded cathode, to the L7805ACV input. This is a proposed hardware selection for instructor review; measure loaded input/output voltage and regulator temperature before integration.

Footprints and mounting: selected ceramic capacitors use 5mm lead spacing; the electrolytic uses 2mm. Verify the power-jack tabs, fuse holder and test loops against manufacturer drawings. Use electrically insulating thermal attachment and strain relief for the probes; check lab stock before purchasing general mounting materials.
