Revision notes

U1 now uses the LM7805T from the lab kit. SW1 is the debug and arm button. J4 and J5 are keyed 2×4 UART connectors. Pin 1 carries data and pin 8 is ground. The team still needs to confirm the cable pinout.

R24–R26 set the unused amplifier and comparator inputs. U4A amplifies the motor current reading by 21, and R6/C8 filter it before the PIC reads it. KiCad reported 0 errors and 0 warnings.
