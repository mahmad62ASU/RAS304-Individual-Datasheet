03 / COMPONENT SELECTION

Chosen with a purpose.

A concrete reference design, with provisional selections and the decisions still needed before a PCB release.

The proposed core.

01

Temperature sensing

Vishay NTCLE100E3103JB0

3 probes · radial thermistor

10 kΩ at 25°C; B25/85 = 3977 K. Low cost and simple ratiometric sensing. The ±5% resistance tolerance makes calibration important.

Do not attach the coated bead directly to live metal. Use an electrically insulating thermal attachment.

Manufacturer datasheet ↗
02

Analog conditioning

Microchip MCP6002-I/P

2 packages · PDIP-8

Three unity-gain buffers drive the ADC filters. Rail-to-rail inputs and outputs suit the 5 V divider range. Configure the fourth channel to a quiet valid input.

1 kΩ series / 100 nF shunt at each ADC pin; local 100 nF bypass per IC.

Manufacturer datasheet ↗
03

Local controller

PIC18F57Q43 Curiosity Nano

1 board · team-standard MCU

12-bit multiplexed ADC, local temperature conversion, invalid-sensor checks, hysteresis and latched shutdown logic.

Proposed RA0 / RA1 / RA2 inputs. Confirm Nano pad map and acquisition timing before layout.

Manufacturer datasheet ↗
04

Logic regulation

ST L7805ACV

1 regulator · TO-220

A-series 5 V linear regulator is selected to support the team’s ±0.2 V rail target under the specified datasheet conditions.

Approximately 0.694 W screening dissipation at nominal 9 V and the 160 mA allocation; enclosure testing required.

Manufacturer datasheet ↗
05

Shared fault output

onsemi P2N2222A

1 transistor · TO-92

Open-collector output sinks the shared FAULT_N line. A removable pullup supports standalone testing.

One agreed bus pullup only. MCU reset and local power loss require master-side inhibit coordination.

Manufacturer datasheet ↗
06

Optional cooling switch

Infineon IRLZ44NPBF

1 MOSFET · TO-220 · optional

Logic-level low-side switch for a future 9 V fan, supplied from the fused 9 V branch.

Fan model, start/stall current, supply-switching compatibility and final protection remain unselected.

Manufacturer datasheet ↗

Resistance becomes temperature.

R(T) = 10,000 · exp[3977 · (1 / (T + 273.15) − 1 / 298.15)]

Vsense = VDD · R(T) / (10,000 + R(T))

The same 5 V rail excites the divider and references the ADC, making the ideal ratio ratiometric. The beta model is an approximation; use the manufacturer’s curve and calibration for final settings.
