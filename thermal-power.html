The thermal resistance is a datasheet screening value, not a prediction of the finished enclosure. Verify the actual input after reverse protection, mounting, ambient temperature, junction limit, and heatsink requirements.

C / Size the whole source.

At nominal 9 V, the logic/regulator branch plans for approximately 166 mA input. Adding a hypothetical 200 mA running fan gives 366 mA before fan startup. The fan is on the 9 V branch and contributes no running load to the 5 V regulator.
