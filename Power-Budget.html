Regulator and source sizing

With the allocation above, 5 V output power is 5 × 0.16576 ≈ 0.829 W. Allowing a provisional 10 mA for L7805CV ground current gives approximately 0.176 A at the 9 V input, or 1.58 W. The selected 9 V, 1 A adapter provides substantial current margin. Replace the 10 mA allowance with the applicable datasheet maximum across input, load and temperature before final review.

Thermal check

At nominal input, regulator dissipation is approximately (9 − 5) × 0.16576 + 9 × 0.010 ≈ 0.753 W. Using a 62.5 °C/W TO-220 junction-to-ambient figure gives an illustrative rise of 47 °C. At 25 °C ambient, this estimates about 72 °C junction temperature. This is a screening calculation, not thermal validation; adapter tolerance, enclosure, airflow and board mounting must be included, and a heatsink added if needed.

Regulator alternatives

The Component Selection page compares three linear regulators: L7805CV, LM317TG and LM2940CT-5.0/NOPB. L7805CV is preferred for the fixed 5 V / 9 V architecture and price. LM317 requires a divider and minimum-load check; LM2940 requires the specified output-capacitor ESR and offers little dropout advantage from 9 V. All three require a dissipation check.

Power-source alternatives and battery life

The source comparison includes Adafruit 9 V / 1 A, Pololu 9 V / 1 A and Pololu 9 V / 5 A adapters. A mains adapter is selected to satisfy the course architecture. Battery lifetime is not applicable to this configuration; no battery capacity or runtime is claimed.
