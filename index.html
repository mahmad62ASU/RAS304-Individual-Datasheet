THE PROJECT

A small board.
A critical responsibility.

Team 103 is developing a modular, computer-connected control system for a two-degree-of-freedom robotic arm.

My responsibility is temperature control: monitor the motor-driver regions and local power stage, identify overheating or invalid sensor readings, and request a coordinated motor shutdown through the shared fault interface.

This datasheet develops a proposed three-sensor thermal board for that system. It brings the sensing, conditioning, firmware behavior, optional cooling, and power planning into one design record.
